# Analysis on Olist marketplace

Olist is an enterprise and SaaS platform that connects small and medium-sized merchants to major online marketplaces. Olist Offers retail technology and a e-commerce ecosystem that helps businesses sell across multiple channels and manage their operations from one central place.

**Tools:** Google BigQuery (SQL), Python (panda), Power BI, matplotlib

[Data from Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce)

# Business Question

>Olist's Head of Operations wants to know what is hurting customer satisfaction, which sellers and regions drive it, and where to act first." Currency is Brazilian reais (R$). Olist believes that the Late deliveries are the driving force of bad reviews, resulting in low customer retention rate and low customer satsifaction. Figure out What should Olist fix first? What steps should Olist take to increase retention and overall customer satsifaction.

# About the data

- 9 related tables
- 99,441 orders
- Data from Sep 2016 to Oct 2018
